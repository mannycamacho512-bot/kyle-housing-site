The Project

What I Built

I built a market intelligence system focused on individual neighborhoods in Kyle, Texas.

Raw MLS records are cleaned, processed and analyzed before being turned into Power BI reports and public-facing market information on my website.

Power BI is the analysis layer — not the final product.

It allows me to break large housing datasets into understandable trends, comparisons and market signals before deciding what information should be published.
