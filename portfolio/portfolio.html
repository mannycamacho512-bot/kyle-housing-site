The Project

What I Built

I built a market intelligence system focused on residential activity in Kyle, Texas, with the ability to break the market down to the individual subdivision level.

Housing data is cleaned, organized and analyzed before being transformed into Power BI analysis, market reports and public-facing neighborhood data on my website.

More than a dashboard.

The project connects data preparation, analysis, visualization and reporting into one system designed to turn large amounts of housing data into information that is easier to understand and use.

Power BI

Making the Data Understandable

Power BI gives me a way to break the underlying housing data into focused views of sales, inventory, pricing, market time and subdivision-level activity.

Data Layer

SQL + Data Warehouse

Structured storage and querying make the same underlying data usable across analysis, historical reporting and visualization.

SQL

SQL provides a repeatable way to query listing and sales records for questions such as subdivision activity, inventory, pricing behavior and historical market trends.

Data Warehouse

Historical market data can be organized into a consistent structure so the same source of information can support Power BI, automated reporting and web-based market data.

Final Output

Published Market Reporting

The website is the reporting layer of the project. Analysis is turned into neighborhood-level statistics, market data and consumer-facing reports that make the underlying information usable.
