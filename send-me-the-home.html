What I Look At Before You Move Forward

Price history: Has it dropped, sat, or just hit the market?
Days on market: Is there urgency, or room to negotiate?
Nearby competition: What else could you buy instead?
Taxes and HOA: Does the payment still make sense?
Subdivision activity: Is that pocket moving or sitting?
Offer angle: Tour, wait, negotiate, ask for credits, or walk?
I don’t just open the door. I look at the deal. My goal is to help you understand what you’re walking into before you get pulled into the next step.
